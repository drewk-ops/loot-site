Loot: Personal Inventory

Privacy Policy

Loot for iOS

Effective date: August 2, 2026

This policy describes how Loot (published as “Loot: Personal Inventory”) handles your information. The app works with the minimum data necessary to function, and stores it on your device.

Data the App Uses

  • Photos — pictures of your belongings that you take with the camera or pick from your photo library, stored locally on your device.
  • Inventory records — item names, categories, tags, notes, quantities, conditions, containers, locations and outfits you create, stored locally on your device.

How the App Uses Data

  • To build your personal inventory.
  • To let you browse, search and organize your belongings.
  • To cut out item photos from their background — this processing happens on your device.

Storage and Sharing

As of the date of this policy:

  • Your data is stored locally on your device.
  • The app does not transmit your data to us or to third parties.
  • The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Photo processing (background removal) runs on your device.

Your Controls

  • View and edit all your data in the app.
  • Delete any item, photo or the whole inventory at any time.
  • Manage camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data.

Children’s Privacy

The app is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the app and on the website with an updated date.
