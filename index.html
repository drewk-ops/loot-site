Loot: RPG Home Inventory

Turn your belongings into a beautiful RPG-style inventory. Snap a photo of an item — it is cut out from the background on your device and lands in your grid.

What Loot does

Everything you own, finally in one place you enjoy opening.

🎒

RPG-style inventory grid

Your belongings laid out as a game inventory — tidy slots, clear icons, satisfying to browse.

✂️

On-device photo cut-outs

Snap an item and it is lifted from its background right on your iPhone, then dropped into your grid.

📦

Containers within containers

Mark a wardrobe, a drawer or a suitcase as a container and nest them as deep as your real life goes.

🗺️

Places for multi-city life

Keep track of what is stored where — the flat you live in, the storage unit in another city, a friend's attic.

🔍

Instant search

Type a word and find the thing — plus the container and the place it currently lives in.

👔

Outfit sets

Save combinations you love and pull the whole look back up whenever you need it.

🌙

Works offline

Your inventory is stored on your device and opens instantly, plane mode included. No account needed.

Your stuff stays your stuff

Photos and records live on your device. Background removal runs on your device. Loot works offline and needs no account — see the Privacy Policy for details.
